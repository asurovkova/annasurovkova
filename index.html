Anna Surovková

Hi! I’m Anna, a Czech Human-Centered Researcher and first-year master’s student in Industrial Design at KAIST. I spent the second half of my bachelor’s studies as an HCI research intern with the Immersive Design Group at the Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech) in Shenzhen, China. My interests span HCI, interaction design, Extended Reality, technology perception, and accessibility. Alongside my studies, I have gained industry experience at companies including Microsoft and ČSOB, where I worked on UX design, accessibility, and emerging technologies.
