Anna Surovková

Hi! I'm Anna, a first-year master's student in Industrial Design at KAIST. Originally from the Czech Republic, I completed my bachelor's degree in Design of Information Services at MUNI. My research interests lie at the intersection of human-computer interaction, emerging technologies, and interactive system design. Currently, I am working as a research intern in the Immersive Design Group at the Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech) in Shenzhen, China, under the supervision of Prof. Seungwoo Je. Alongside my studies, I have gained industry experience at companies including Microsoft and ČSOB, where I worked on UX design, accessibility, and emerging technologies.
