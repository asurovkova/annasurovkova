Selected Work

// Accessible AR research

SoundWeAR

An AR system co-designed with Deaf and hard-of-hearing participants. It filters environmental sounds and anchors clear visual cues in the user’s field of view to support outdoor awareness, safety, and control.

  • HCI research
  • Co-design
  • CHI 2026

// Spatial interaction

Coffee Batch in XR

A spatial redesign of the Filtru brewing guide. It replaces a small, auto-locking phone screen with hands-free AR guidance, preparation previews, and step-by-step cues that remain visible while making coffee.

  • AR/XR
  • Interaction design
  • Spatial prototyping

// Service design

LifeSaver

A research-led first-aid service for adults aged 50–65. Short workplace training and a microlearning app help people refresh outdated knowledge regularly and build the confidence to act in an emergency.

  • Service design
  • User research
  • Microlearning

// UX research & redesign

Záchranka

A usability study and homepage redesign of the Czech emergency app. The study identified unclear functions, fear of accidental calls, and stress-induced navigation problems; the redesign strengthens hierarchy, labels, and confidence in critical moments.

  • UX research
  • Interaction design
  • Accessibility

// Accessible applied AI

Rotty

A Microsoft-recognized mobile assistant helping people with visual impairments identify spoiled produce and sort bio-waste. An Azure Custom Vision classifier, text-to-speech navigation, and practical disposal guidance make food-waste decisions more accessible.

  • Applied AI
  • Accessibility
  • Sustainability
