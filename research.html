// Research

Expanding how people perceive, create, and interact.

I study how spatial and multimodal interfaces can make information easier to perceive, interpret, and act on in real-world contexts.

My work combines user research, co-design, prototyping, and evaluation across Extended Reality, sensing, and accessible interaction.

Core question
How can emerging technologies expand human perception and interaction?
Research interests
HCI // Interaction Design // Extended Reality // Technology Perception // Accessibility
Outcome
Clearer, more accessible interaction

Research Directions

Extended Reality

Spatial interfaces that connect digital information with real-world places, objects, and activities.

Sensing & Multimodal Interaction

Transforming invisible information into cues people can see, hear, and feel.

Accessibility & Inclusive Design

Co-designing systems that reduce barriers and support diverse ways of perceiving.
